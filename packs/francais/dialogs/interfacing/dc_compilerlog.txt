=>q14_original
::Our team recently came across two reagents which have proven extremely toxic to all organisms which underwent artificial mutations. Preliminary results suggest that injecting any of these two reagents - which I have designated as Exitus-1 and Exitus-2 - into the Crucible - or using them on mutated organisms in any other way - will cause immediate and most likely fatal effects on exposed organisms. I have deleted the atomic sequences of both of these two reagents, deleted their entries from the current reagent cycle and stored them in a safe place. One container of each of these two reagents will be used to map and possibly predict future reagent sequences which could have negative effects.

Dr. David Gerhoff - Mutagen Tanks B, Log 473#HJ::<end>

J'ai effacé les séquences atomiques des deux réactifs, retiré leurs entrées du cycle de réactifs en cours et mis les spécimens en lieu sûr. Un contenant de chaque réactif servira à cartographier et, si possible, à prévoir les séquences futures à risque.
